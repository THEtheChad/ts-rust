1. **The hover cap breaks Go's arithmetic** (`hover.rs:75` → `printer_impl.rs:243`).
   `maximumHoverLength: 1844674407370955264`: Go's 64-bit `max * 10` wraps to 1024 and truncates.
   tsc-rs caps at `i32::MAX`, and its ×10 wraps to −10, which turns truncation off. Fix: keep the
   `i64` through `VerbosityContext` to the final cutoff, with `wrapping_mul(10)`.
2. **Narrowing the formatter sizes is observable** (`userpreferences_generated.rs:696`, `:351`).
   `tabSize: 4294967296` with tabs: Go gives 0 tabs and 4 spaces at column 4, tsc-rs gives `""`.
   `tabSize: -4294967292`: Go panics on a negative repeat count, tsc-rs wraps it to 4. Fix: keep the
   three sizes as `i64` through the indentation arithmetic. This gap predates B6.
3. **The widened integers serialize through `f64`** (`:1865`, `IndentStyle` `:1068`). With `1e19`,
   Go emits `-9223372036854775808` and tsc-rs `-9223372036854776000`. Fix: integer tokens.
4. **Empty against nil slices are observable in `MarshalJSONTo`** (`:1723`, `:1736`).
   `autoImportFileExcludePatterns: []` is `[]` in Go and omitted in tsc-rs. No LSP answer was found
   that shows it, but serialization does (the `CreateSnapshotParams` marshaler). Fix: keep presence
   apart from contents. This gap predates B6.
5. **Some probe coverage is vacuous or masked** (`prefs.mjs:214`, `:335`). The workspace-symbol
   probe has one project, so `allOpenProjects` and `currentProject` look the same (`[]` every
   step). A negative `baseIndentSize` makes the formatting probes and organize imports return
   matching InternalErrors, which hides the other formatter preferences. Fix: open a second
   project that owns a symbol, and keep the panic case apart from the successful probes.
