   Delete→Create, but the derived `Ord` plus `sort_unstable()` reorders them to
   Create→Delete, and `OverlayFS::process_changes` then coalesces the pair to

   `Reconciled::default()` after the views have left `PENDING`
   (`mod.rs:139`, `mod.rs:372`). Needs an explicit failure result plus
   fallback or retry.

   counters and timing, the phase-timing machinery, `Slot::by_worker`, and
   timestamps taken even with timing off. A build without the feature still
   prints `GOPORT_LOAD_TIMES` output.
5. **Should-fix: reconciliation concurrency has no aggregate bound.** Each load
   adds a coordinator and up to 16 threads, keeping its whole view alive.
   Needs a bounded queue with generations and cancellation, without losing
   observations.
