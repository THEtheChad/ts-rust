## What it does

`GOPORT_PARTITION_MONITOR=<file>` appends JSON lines: capped event lines,
milestone lines (the first time each peak crosses a value) and one summary
per checker at exit, with peaks and per-kind counts. Margins are applied
offline from the peaks. `GOPORT_PM_MARGINS` sets the margins of the
`margin` event lines.

**Cold-depth shadow** (the answer to Astra finding 8). Each instantiation
frame records, for a result type it creates, the height its computation
needed, counting the heights of cached results it used. When a frame
returns a type that existed before the current file's check, it was a cache
hit on another file's work: its recorded height is added to the current
depth. The result, `vdepth`, is the depth a checker that lacked that work
would reach.
- Type-parameter frames, which only read a mapper, add nothing.
- Same-file hits add to heights (for transitivity) but not to `vdepth`.

## Results

- **Real data:** 660 project runs (10 projects × 22 configs × noemit, emit
  and extdiag × 8 settings, faststeal ×3) and 11,568 corpus runs (1,446
  checked cases × 8). 27 divergent runs, all zod/zod. Every one fired, under
  the limit class alone, at every margin up to the hard limits. No misses.
  The corpus has 0 divergent runs and 0 limit or shadow fires.
- **Fixtures:** `chain-one-warmer` reproduces Astra's case. Go and c4 report
  TS5115; c1 and c2 report nothing, and their real peak depth is 24. The
  limit monitor misses it at any margin (the warm peak is about 100/k for k
  segments). The shadow sees 124 and fires.
- **Fire rate on clean configs (limits + shadow):** 3 of 8 large configs at
  margin 50, 1 of 8 (zod) at the hard limits. The 3 at margin 50 are zod,
  t3code/server (per-statement count 3.9M of 5M) and t3code/web (relation
  depth 93 of 100), and both t3code quantities are stable across splits.
- **Cycles, cross-file diagnostics and id tie-breaks** never coincided with
  a divergence, but they fire too often to be triggers: push_type_resolution
  cycles in 4 of 10 projects, cross-file diagnostics in 5, `type_id_tie` in
  93% of corpus runs.
- **Passive:** `parity.sh quick` with the monitor on gives PARITY OK (0 lost
  of 142,396 items).
- **Overhead** (user instructions, `--singleThreaded`, 3 interleaved runs,
  stable 1.95.0 builds): off +0.18% to +0.35%, on +2.5% to +5.2%.
