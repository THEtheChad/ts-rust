# W3: the last complexity targets (code-quality phase)

Lane W3 works through the targets that remained after wave 2, following
`w2-brief.md`.

- **Base:** `perf/first-principles` @ `837456235`.
- **Targets:** from `quality.sh ranking` at the base:
  - 14 port-bloat rows with Δcog ≥ 10 against Go (2 of them at ≥ 25);
  - 1 row with ratio ≥ 1.5 and Rust cog > 25 (`new_type_response`, also among the 14);
  - 24 own-code functions over 25 (5 over 50);
  - 12 ambiguous name matches.
- **Excluded:** files that lanes B6 and B7 own, and the harness. None of the
  targets is in those files.

Cognitive figures are rust-code-analysis's figures for Rust and gocog's for Go
(`docs/perf-rearch/quality.md`). "Residue" means Go's tagless `switch`
written as an else-if chain. The w2 brief accepts that, by root's decision of
2026-11-09: each `else` scores +1 in Rust, and Go's `case` labels score
nothing.
